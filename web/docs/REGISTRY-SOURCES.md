# Bonus public records where available

The publisher catalog records fixed official source endpoints and the event each dataset can substantiate. It is not an enable switch or a coverage promise. This change consolidates unchanged publisher endpoints, jurisdiction and category descriptors, field mappings, and request limits used by the two existing research adapters. It does not enable sources, expand coverage, change lead counts, or add a bonus to reports.

| Current catalog metadata | What the source can show |
| --- | --- |
| [Austin issued construction permits](https://data.austintexas.gov/Permitting/Issued-Construction-Permits/3syk-w9eu) | A permit issuance, not hiring or a purchase |
| [Vancouver business licences](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/assets/business-licences/) | A licence issuance, not necessarily a business opening |
| [UK Food Standards Agency ratings](https://ratings.food.gov.uk/open-data) | A hygiene record, not a permit or licence; metadata only |

Existing optional research mode, adapter conditions, result limits and fallback behavior remain unchanged. A missing dataset or an empty result says nothing about the market or whether there are buyers.

Before an independently approved bonus receipt can run, it needs matching authenticated review and immutable admitted scope for one exact tenant/job/attempt and canonical geography/category/event, a unique execution claim, and a current-attempt receipt write that refuses stale attempts. Each publisher record must independently confirm the ID, event and date in the requested window. Keep any eventual bonus read-only and separate from lead counts and contact/outreach decisions.
