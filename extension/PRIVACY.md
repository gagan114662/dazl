# dazl Connect: privacy

dazl Connect reads, in the browser it is installed in, only the LinkedIn pages your dazl workspace asks for: company People pages,
profiles and post comments. It opens each one in a background tab with your own LinkedIn sign-in, reads the names, job titles and
profile links shown on it, sends them only to the dazl site you connected it to (dazl.ai or staging.dazl.ai) over HTTPS, and closes
the tab. dazl sets the pace: your reading hours, one page at a time, a pause of 45 to 180 seconds between pages, a daily cap, and a
full day's stop whenever LinkedIn shows a security check or asks to slow down.

It never reads or sends your cookies (it has no cookie permission), and dazl's servers never sign in to LinkedIn. It never connects,
messages, likes, follows or posts. It does not read your browsing history or any other site, and does not track you or show ads.

On this device it stores only the dazl site it is paired with, a device token, and what it last did.

To stop: **Pause** or **Disconnect** in dazl Settings, **Forget this browser** in the extension's popup, or remove the extension.
