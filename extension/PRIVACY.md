# dazl Connect: privacy

dazl Connect reads two cookies of linkedin.com (`li_at` and `JSESSIONID`) from the browser it is installed in, and sends them
only to the dazl site you connected it to (dazl.ai or staging.dazl.ai), over HTTPS. It does this when you click **Connect
LinkedIn** in dazl, and again when LinkedIn changes either cookie.

It does not read your browsing history, your tabs, the content of any page, or any other site's cookies. It does not track you,
show ads, or send anything to anyone other than dazl.

On this device it stores only the dazl site it is paired with and a device token (in the extension's own storage). dazl stores the
LinkedIn session encrypted for your workspace and uses it only for the LinkedIn reads you switch on in dazl; it never posts,
likes, messages or connects on your behalf.

To stop: click **Disconnect** in dazl Settings (the session is deleted and the device token stops working), or **Forget this
browser** in the extension's popup, or remove the extension.

Questions: the contact address on https://dazl.ai.
